HEATHENS!

#HeathensTuuko #MunguNiWetu

Platinum Credit Heathens is a rugby club based at Kyadondo Rugby Club Kampala,Uganda.It is a male team basing on the age of above 20.This team has managed to attain so many accomplishments over the years as you can see below.

  • 15 times Nile Special Rugby Premiership League Champions!
  • Uganda Cup Champions 2015!
  • 2024 Champions of the Nile Special Rugby Championship!
  • Uganda Cup Champions2016!

TOYOTA BUFFALOES

"One Team,One Spirit,One Win"

Toyota Buffaloes is a rugby club based at Kyadondo Rugby Club Kampala,Uganda.It is a male team as well that also bases on the age of 2o and above ie it is an adult team. Buffaloes is sponsored by the Toyota and it has been funding it for a number if years now.

THUNDERBIRDS

"Thunderbirds..Attitude"!

Thunderbirds is a club based at Kyadondo Rugby Club in Kampala,Uganda.It is a female team that is also age based.it deals with players of age 20 and above.This team was founded in 2003 ad it is the oldest and most successful womens rugby club in Uganda.It is known for dominance both in 15s and 7s formats. It frequently competes in the Womens Premiership and National 7s series.This club is a key to nurturing Ugandan female rugby talent and the Ladycranes players.Thunderbirds team is known for alot of achievements and impact towards Kyadondo Rugby Club as shown by the differnt records below.

  • Oldest Womens rugby club in Uganda.
  • Most Successfu by trophies.
  • 2025 National 7s series champions.
  • 2024 Kyadondo 7s hosts.
  • Gulu 7s champions 2024.
  • 2025 7s series champions
